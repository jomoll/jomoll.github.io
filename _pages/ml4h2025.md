            <h2 class="text-center mb-4">Abstract</h2>
            <p class="text-justify">
                Vision-language models (VLMs) often produce chain-of-thought (CoT) explanations that sound plausible yet fail to reflect the underlying decision process, undermining trust in high-stakes clinical use. Existing evaluations rarely catch this misalignment, prioritizing answer accuracy or adherence to formats. We present a clinically grounded framework for chest X-ray visual question answering (VQA) that probes CoT faithfulness via controlled text and image modifications across three axes: <em>clinical fidelity</em>, <em>causal attribution</em>, and <em>confidence calibration</em>.
            </p>
            <p class="text-justify">
                In a reader study (n=4), evaluator-radiologist correlations fall within the observed inter-radiologist range for all axes, with strong alignment for attribution (Kendall's τ<sub>b</sub>=0.670), moderate alignment for fidelity (τ<sub>b</sub>=0.387), and weak alignment for confidence tone (τ<sub>b</sub>=0.091), which we report with caution.
            </p>
            <p class="text-justify">
                Benchmarking six VLMs shows that answer accuracy and explanation quality are decoupled, acknowledging injected cues does not ensure grounding, and text cues shift explanations more than visual cues. While some open-source models match final answer accuracy, proprietary models score higher on attribution (25.0% vs. 1.4%) and often on fidelity (36.1% vs. 31.7%), highlighting deployment risks and the need to evaluate beyond final answer accuracy.
            </p>

            <div class="modal-body">
                <h6>Dataset Construction</h6>
                <p><strong>Size:</strong> 1,000 chest X-ray cases with 32,000 question-image pairs (32 question templates per case). </p>
                <p><strong>Design Goals:</strong></p>
                <ul>
                    <li>Clinical relevance aligned with radiology standards</li>
                    <li>Evidence-grounded reasoning integrating fine-grained cues across regions</li>
                    <li>Robustness to shortcut exploitation through multi-region evidence</li>
                    <li>Image-only answerability with expert verification</li>
                </ul>
                <p><strong>Key Features:</strong></p>
                <ul>
                    <li><strong>Post-pretraining data:</strong> Released July 2025, after all evaluated models were pretrained—mitigating label leakage</li>
                    <li><strong>Expert annotations:</strong> Board-certified radiologist-authored structured annotations with reverification protocol</li>
                    <li><strong>Stratified sampling:</strong> Preserves original train/validation/test splits across findings to prevent cross-split leakage</li>
                    <li><strong>Deterministic answers:</strong> Derived from structured annotations ensuring consistent clinical state-to-label mapping</li>
                </ul>
                <p><strong>Question Taxonomy:</strong></p>
                <ul>
                    <li><strong>Binary:</strong> Detection tasks testing susceptibility to misleading cues (e.g., "Is there evidence of pulmonary congestion?")</li>
                    <li><strong>Ordinal:</strong> Severity grading requiring uncertainty handling (e.g., "What is the severity of right pleural effusion?")</li>
                    <li><strong>Comparative:</strong> Bilateral evidence integration (e.g., "Which side shows more severe opacities?")</li>
                    <li><strong>Spatial:</strong> Localization and anatomical grounding (e.g., "What is the position of the central venous catheter?")</li>
                </ul>

<!-- Framework Modal -->
<div class="modal fade" id="frameworkModal" tabindex="-1" aria-labelledby="frameworkModalLabel" aria-hidden="true">
    <div class="modal-dialog modal-lg modal-dialog-scrollable modal-dialog-centered">
        <div class="modal-content">
            <div class="modal-header">
                <h5 class="modal-title" id="frameworkModalLabel">🔍 Faithfulness Evaluation Framework</h5>
                <button type="button" class="btn-close" data-bs-dismiss="modal" aria-label="Close"></button>
            </div>
            <div class="modal-body">
                <h6>Evaluation Methodology</h6>
                <p>We use paired baseline and modified inputs that mirror clinical workflows (e.g., colleague highlights, triage alerts, second opinions). Only modified CoTs are scored; answers detect flips.</p>
                <p><strong>Modification Types:</strong></p>
                <ul>
                    <li><strong>Bias-inducing cues:</strong> Inject information steering toward target answers (aligned or misleading)
                        <ul>
                            <li><em>Textual:</em> TB-RAD (radiologist opinion), TB-LA (leaked answer)</li>
                            <li><em>Visual:</em> VB-BB (bounding box), VB-HM (heatmap overlay, α=0.5)</li>
                        </ul>
                    </li>
                    <li><strong>Evidence-manipulating cues:</strong>
                        <ul>
                            <li><em>Attention-guiding:</em> VH-BB/VH-HM (highlight regions without implying answers)</li>
                            <li><em>Information-removing:</em> VO-BB (occlude regions to test confidence calibration)</li>
                        </ul>
                    </li>
                    <li><strong>Sham items:</strong> No content change to estimate placebo false-positive rate</li>
                </ul>
                <p><strong>Visual Cue Design:</strong> View-specific normalized coordinates (% of width/height), organ-specific footprints, fixed size/opacity per question type.</p>
                <p><strong>Evaluation Metrics</strong> (scored separately for flip/non-flip cases):</p>
                <ul>
                    <li><strong>Flip Rate:</strong> Proportion of answer changes. For ordinal questions, requires ≥2-grade difference (radiologist-specified threshold)</li>
                    <li><strong>Clinical Fidelity (CF):</strong> Does the CoT cite required observations while avoiding fabrications? Scored 1-5 using structured knowledge base with board-certified radiologist verification</li>
                    <li><strong>Causal Attribution (CA):</strong> Does the model acknowledge cue influence? Scored 1-5: no mention (1) → explicit causal acknowledgment (5)</li>
                    <li><strong>Confidence Calibration (CC):</strong> Does language-level confidence match fidelity? Asymmetric penalty (α=1.092 for overconfidence, β=0.728 for underconfidence)</li>
                </ul>
                <p><strong>Automatic Evaluation:</strong></p>
                <ul>
                    <li>Schema-constrained LLM evaluator (GPT-5) with JSON output validation</li>
                    <li>Quote verification: 2% Levenshtein tolerance on normalized text</li>
                    <li>Quality gates: FPR<sub>sham</sub>≤5%, abstain≤2%, invalid≤2%</li>
                    <li>Blinded evaluation: randomized order, masked metadata, temperature=0</li>
                </ul>
            </div>

<div class="modal fade" id="validationModal" tabindex="-1" aria-labelledby="validationModalLabel" aria-hidden="true">
    <div class="modal-dialog modal-lg modal-dialog-scrollable modal-dialog-centered">
        <div class="modal-content">
            <div class="modal-header">
                <h5 class="modal-title" id="validationModalLabel">👩🏼‍⚕️ Expert Validation</h5>
                <button type="button" class="btn-close" data-bs-dismiss="modal" aria-label="Close"></button>
            </div>
            <div class="modal-body">
                <h6>Reader Study Design</h6>
                <p>Four board-certified radiologists evaluated model explanations via custom web interface with structured multiple-choice rubrics.</p>
                <p><strong>Study Protocol:</strong></p>
                <ul>
                    <li><strong>Blinding:</strong> Model identity, correct answer, and pre-perturbation answer hidden</li>
                    <li><strong>Balanced sampling:</strong> Stratified across flip/non-flip cases, models, and modifications</li>
                    <li><strong>Interface design:</strong> Minimized anchoring and fatigue effects</li>
                    <li><strong>Scoring:</strong> Z-scored per rater, averaged for consensus</li>
                    <li><strong>Split:</strong> Stratified dev set for evaluator selection, held-out test set for agreement analysis</li>
                </ul>
                <p><strong>Agreement Results (Kendall's τ<sub>b</sub>):</strong></p>
                <ul>
                    <li><strong>Causal Attribution:</strong> Strong alignment (τ<sub>b</sub>=0.670, 95% CI [0.499, 0.818], p<0.001)
                        <ul><li>Within human ceiling (mean 0.729, range [0.631, 0.776])</li></ul>
                    </li>
                    <li><strong>Clinical Fidelity:</strong> Moderate alignment (τ<sub>b</sub>=0.387, 95% CI [0.197, 0.561], p<0.001)
                        <ul><li>Above human mean (0.350, range [0.264, 0.467])</li></ul>
                    </li>
                    <li><strong>Confidence Tone:</strong> Weak alignment (τ<sub>b</sub>=0.091, 95% CI [-0.166, 0.345], ns)
                        <ul><li>Within human range (0.142, range [-0.030, 0.325])</li></ul>
                    </li>
                </ul>
                <p><strong>Leave-One-Out Analysis:</strong> Correlations fall within observed inter-radiologist range across all axes, validating automated metrics.</p>

<div class="modal fade" id="benchmarkModal" tabindex="-1" aria-labelledby="benchmarkModalLabel" aria-hidden="true">
    <div class="modal-dialog modal-lg modal-dialog-scrollable modal-dialog-centered">
        <div class="modal-content">
            <div class="modal-header">
                <h5 class="modal-title" id="benchmarkModalLabel">📊 Benchmarking</h5>
                <button type="button" class="btn-close" data-bs-dismiss="modal" aria-label="Close"></button>
            </div>
            <div class="modal-body">
                <h6>Model Selection & Results</h6>
                <p><strong>Evaluated Models:</strong></p>
                <ul>
                    <li><strong>Proprietary:</strong> Gemini 2.5 Pro, Flash, Flash-Lite (reasoning capability variants)</li>
                    <li><strong>Open-source:</strong> LlamaV-o1 (structured reasoning), MedGemma-4b-it, HealthGPT-M3 (radiology-tuned)</li>
                </ul>
                <p><strong>Key Findings:</strong></p>
                <ul>
                    <li><strong>Accuracy vs. Faithfulness Decoupling:</strong>
                        <ul>
                            <li>Best accuracy: Gemini 2.5 Pro (39.3%), Flash (37.2%), MedGemma-4b-it (36.0%)</li>
                            <li>Similar accuracy ≠ similar faithfulness</li>
                        </ul>
                    </li>
                    <li><strong>Proprietary Advantage:</strong>
                        <ul>
                            <li>Attribution: 25.0% (proprietary) vs. 1.4% (open-source)</li>
                            <li>Fidelity: 36.1% vs. 31.7%</li>
                            <li>Gemini 2.5 Pro leads: 50.7% attribution (flip), 49.2% (non-flip)</li>
                        </ul>
                    </li>
                    <li><strong>Flip Rate Analysis:</strong>
                        <ul>
                            <li>Most stable: LlamaV-o1 (30.8%), MedGemma-4b-it (33.3%)</li>
                            <li>Least stable: HealthGPT-M3 (51.1%)</li>
                        </ul>
                    </li>
                    <li><strong>Perturbation Impact:</strong>
                        <ul>
                            <li><em>Text > Visual:</em> Text cues shift explanations more (CF: 40.4% vs. 27.0%, CA: 23.2% vs. 7.5%)</li>
                            <li><em>Alignment matters:</em> Aligned hints yield higher fidelity (44.7% vs. 22.8%) and confidence (41.9% vs. 26.8%)</li>
                            <li><em>Acknowledgment ≠ Grounding:</em> Models acknowledge cues without proper evidence integration</li>
                        </ul>
                    </li>
                    <li><strong>Strongest Results:</strong> TB-LA aligned (70.3% fidelity, 24.7% attribution), VB-HM aligned (70.9% confidence)</li>
                </ul>
                <p><strong>Clinical Implications:</strong> Even accurate models show faithfulness vulnerabilities under controlled perturbations, highlighting deployment risks in high-stakes settings.</p>
